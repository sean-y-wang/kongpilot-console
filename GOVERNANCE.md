# Governance

KongPilot is currently maintained by [@sean-y-wang](https://github.com/sean-y-wang) (lead maintainer).

- **Decisions** are made by the maintainers in public issues and pull requests. Significant design decisions are recorded as [ADRs](docs/decisions/README.md).
- **Becoming a maintainer:** sustained, high-quality contributions and reviews; invited by existing maintainers.
- **Releases** follow the [release process](docs/development/README.md#release-process).
- **Conduct** is governed by the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
